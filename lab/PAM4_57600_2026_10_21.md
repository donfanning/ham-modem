# PAM-4 57.6 kbit/s VoIP-native mode — 2026-10-21

`modems/pam.py`: G3RUH's chain with two bits per symbol: HDLC -> NRZI -> scrambler -> Gray-coded levels
{-3,-1,+1,+3} -> raised cosine (beta 0.5). `pam57600` = 28800 sym/s on 48 kHz L16 legs (band 0-21.6 kHz);
`pam19200` = 9600 sym/s on 16 kHz legs. Not an on-air ham waveform; legacy apps see a fast KISS/TNC-2/AGW TNC.

Receiver: interpolate to 5 samples/symbol, image-rejection low-pass flat to the band edge, slow DC removal,
Gardner timing and a symmetric |y| level tracker updated per 16-symbol block (vectorised), Gray slicing at 0 and
+/-2A, vectorised descrambler and NRZI, per-bit HDLC.

## Bugs found on the way (all in the receiver's acquisition, not visible with continuous signal)

1. Silence between bursts let the level estimate decay to ~0; the level-normalised Gardner error then exploded
   on filter-tail residue and slammed the period to its limit. Fix: squelch -- hold timing in silence.
2. An asymmetric fast-attack level tracker settles *above* the mean of |y| and mis-slices the +/-3A symbols
   (0/40). Fix: symmetric average, fast only for the first symbols after the squelch opens.
3. DC removal over 64 symbols wobbled by ~0.28A with the data (sqrt(5)A/8) and closed the eye (per-symbol trace
   showed mean decision error 0.25A on clean audio). Fix: 2048-symbol window. Harmless for 2-level G3RUH,
   fatal for 4 levels.

## Results (40 frames of ~250 bytes, offline)

| | clean | gain x0.5 | -300 ppm (band-limited) | noise 1000 | 1300 | 1600 rms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pam57600, block 16, KP 0.6 | 40 | 40 | 40 (+500 rms noise) | 40 | 39 | 35 |
| pam19200 @ 16 kHz | 40 | 40 | 39 | 35 | | |

Block/gain sweep: 32-symbol blocks lost noise margin (33/40 at 1000 rms), 8-symbol blocks cost 21% CPU for no
gain. CPU at 57.6k: ~8% (clean) to ~12% (noisy) of one core, down from ~17% per-symbol; the remaining per-bit
HDLC loop is the next thing to vectorise.
