# Phase 10.1 / 10.2: native ARDOP physical layer (4FSK, 4PSK, 8PSK, 16QAM) — 2026-10-22

`hammodem/ardop/` — `codec.py` (frame table, CRC16/CRC8, Rockliff-convention RS, Packed6, session ID),
`modulate.py` (encoders + waveforms), `demod.py` (`Receiver`). Ported for interop from ardopcf (MIT; Muething,
Wiseman, LaRue). Reference recordings: `lab/ardop/gen_ref.py` (one fresh ardopcf process per frame,
`--writetxwav`, labelled by ardopcf's own `--decodewav`); a subset is in `tests/fixtures/ardopcf/`.

## Transmitter: sample-exact with ardopcf

Re-modulating the bytes decoded from each ardopcf recording reproduces the recording to within **±6 LSB**
(residual −76 to −84 dB) for every 4FSK, PSK and QAM mode, including leader, sync, frame type, data, trailer and
TX filter. Three details beyond the data symbols were needed to get there:

| detail | ardopcf | before |
|---|---|---|
| TX filter | frequency-sampling filter (comb 1 − r¹²⁰z⁻¹²⁰, 1 − r²z⁻², resonators on 100 Hz slots with fixed transition weights, ×1/120), 200/500/1000/2000 Hz; an FIR of 123 taps (the comb zeros cancel the poles), ported as its impulse response | ordinary windowed-sinc band-pass |
| output alignment | first 60 output samples dropped, no flush at the end | full length |
| trailer | `1 + trailer_ms/10` symbols of the unscaled 1500 Hz PSK template (phase 0), filtered | silence |

PSK/QAM: 100 bd (120 samples), carriers 1500 | 1400,1600 | 1200–1800 | 800–2200 (200 Hz steps, skipping 1500);
symbol `26000·sin(πk/119)·sin(2πfk/12000 + φ)`; one phase-0 reference symbol per carrier, then differential
phase (`prev + sym·SymSet`, SymSet 2 for 4PSK), 16QAM bit 3 = half amplitude; carriers summed, scaled 1.2 / 0.65
(0.67 for 16QAM) / 0.4 / 0.25 (0.27) and soft-clipped above 30000.

## Interop

| direction | result |
|---|---|
| ardopcf → ours: all 18 data modes (both E/O where recorded), ConReq, Ping, IDFrame (28 recordings) | all decode, CRC OK |
| ours → ardopcf `--decodewav`: every data mode E and O (incl. 600 bd, full and short payloads), ConReq, ConAck, Ping, IDFrame, DataACK | all PASS |

600 baud "FM only" frames (tones 600/1200/1800/2400 Hz, 20-sample symbols, 2000 Hz filter): 4FSK.2000.600 sends its
600 data bytes as three blocks of 200 + 50 RS, but 4FSK.2000.600S is a single 200 + 50 block (ardopcf splits only
when data_len >= 600). ardopcf itself corrects 2–16 bytes on clean 600 bd frames (ours and its own); we correct 0.

Fine timing: |correlation| with the leader peaks every 4 samples (half a 1500 Hz cycle), which left the frame start
up to 4 samples off — harmless at 50/100 bd, a fifth of a symbol at 600 bd (it failed a round trip at 300 rms noise).
The sync pair (last two leader symbols) is always positive, so a signed correlation over it now picks the right
carrier phase: timing error 0 in 14/14 noisy trials at varied offsets.

**ardopcf's own receiver is marginal on full multi-carrier 8PSK/16QAM frames.** With our (sample-exact)
waveforms, ardopcf corrects 0 bytes on 1–2 carrier frames but 8–35 bytes on 4/8-carrier 8PSK and 30–100 on 4/8
carrier 16QAM when all carriers carry dense data; on dense text one 16QAM.2000 frame even FAILed. Its own reference
recordings fix only 6–8 because the payload was ~120 bytes padded with zeros: with ardopcf's own payload our frame
gets exactly the same 8 fixes, and a 10-byte payload 2. So this is ardopcf's demodulator, not our transmitter —
worth knowing for gear-shifting thresholds in 10.3 (ardopcf peers will NAK dense 16QAM.2000 often).

## Native receiver vs noise

Gaussian noise over the full 0–6 kHz band (SNR below is wideband; in-band SNR is higher), 5 random full frames
each:

| mode | 25 / 21 / 19 dB | ~15–12 dB | ~11–8 dB | ~9–5 dB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4FSK.500.100 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 (13.3 dB) |
| 4PSK.500.100, 8PSK.500.100 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 (8.8 dB) |
| 16QAM.500.100 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 (6.9 dB) |
| 4PSK.2000.100 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 (6.8 dB) |
| 16QAM.2000.100 | 4/5 (17.5 dB) | 1/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 |

16QAM.2000 is the weak spot (smallest per-carrier margin plus clipping); improvements for later: magnitude
decision relative to a running per-carrier average instead of the reference symbol alone, soft decisions and RS
erasures for low-confidence symbols.

## On audio legs: resampling and streaming (`hammodem/ardop/stream.py`)

`Phy(fs)`: `transmit(encoded)` modulates at 12 kHz and resamples to the leg (streaming rational Kaiser-sinc
resampler, 0.9 of Nyquist); `feed(samples)` resamples to 12 kHz and returns frames as they complete, from blocks of
any size. The receiver reports a frame it is still inside as `truncated` with the buffer length it `need`s, and
`Phy` keeps just enough history (the leader from where it was detected, or ~8 leader symbols before a found frame
type) and otherwise trims to ~1900 samples.

Every data mode (E/O, random full payloads), ConReq, DataACK and IDFrame, streamed in 20 ms blocks:

| leg | frames | result |
|---|---|---|
| 8 kHz PCMU (µ-law) | 39 | all OK, **0 RS bytes corrected** (incl. 16QAM.2000 and 600 bd) |
| 8 kHz PCMA, 16 kHz L16, 48 kHz L16 | 12 each | all OK |

CPU: ~1.2% of real time (1.8 s for 152 s of audio). Two streaming bugs found on the way, both from trimming history
too eagerly: (1) while the buffer ended inside a leader, `find_frame` returned "nothing" and the scan point slid
past the leader start, so detection resumed late in the leader and the 3-symbol timing refinement straddled the
frame-type symbols (frame starts 24–56 samples off, 5/36 frames lost); (2) after the sync was found but before the
frame was complete, the leader was trimmed, so the frame could not be re-found (0 frames). Whole-buffer timing was
exact throughout.
