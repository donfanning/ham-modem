# Passband QAM for 16 kHz legs: 38.4 / 51.2 kbit/s — 2026-10-21

`modems/qam.py`: 6400 sym/s, 4 kHz carrier, RRC beta 0.2 (0.16-7.84 kHz), 64-QAM (`qam38400`) or 256-QAM
(`qam51200`), Gray-coded square constellations. Each burst: 128-symbol QPSK preamble (two x^7+x^6+1 LFSRs), then
G3RUH-scrambled HDLC/FX.25 bits. Receiver: x4 to 64 kHz, mix down, matched RRC; energy squelch; preamble by
correlation (timing + phase + gain), carrier offset from the preamble phase slope, LMS training on the known
symbols; then Mueller-Mueller timing per 16-symbol block, one-tap complex LMS per symbol (gain/phase) plus a
second-order frequency loop; vectorised descrambler + HDLC. No equaliser needed on a linear VoIP path.

## Bugs found while building it

1. Preamble search window too short: the onset estimate can precede the preamble by up to ~2 blocks + filter
   delays (found by comparing a perfect offline correlation, 0.998, with the failing streaming one).
2. Gardner timing barely works at beta 0.2 -> switched to decision-directed Mueller-Mueller; first with the
   sign inverted (higher loop gain made clean audio *worse* -- the tell for positive feedback).
3. NaN: the normalised LMS divided by ~0 in the silence after a burst; and with 1000-sample input blocks the
   "look back one block" onset landed in pure silence where the correlation was exactly 0. Fixes: hold LMS on
   tiny samples, onset from the first above-squelch input sample, reject zero-energy windows.
4. Input blocks longer than a burst hid the next burst (burst end counted in blocks). Input is now processed
   in <= 10 ms chunks and silence is counted in samples.
5. 256-QAM failed at +/-500 ppm: carrier offset (2 Hz at 4 kHz) rotates outer points out of their cells while
   a one-tap LMS lags. Added a frequency loop and, decisively, an initial estimate from the preamble phase slope.
   (On a pure VoIP path the carrier cannot drift -- samples are never resampled; clock differences show up as
   packet slips -- but resampling paths exist.)

## Results

Offline, 20 frames of ~210 bytes: clean / noise 30, 100, 200 rms / gain 0.5 / +200 / -300 ppm all 20/20 except
64-QAM at 200 rms (19/20); bad FCS 0. Both at -500..+500 ppm: 6/6. Multi-frame 0.4 s burst at +/-300 ppm: 7/7.
Noise limit: 64-QAM needs ~30 dB SNR, 256-QAM ~31 dB (a linear L16 leg offers far more). CPU ~6% of a core.

Live, room 7316 (16 kHz ConfBridge), 3 stations x 8 frames: qam38400 48/48, qam51200 48/48 (bad FCS 0).
3% simulated loss, 10 frames each: qam51200 58/60, with --repeat 2 59/60 (single runs).
