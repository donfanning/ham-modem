# Phase 9: Direwolf-compatible 2400 QPSK / 4800 8PSK, IL2P at 300 bd — 2026-10-22

`modems/psk.py` (`psk2400`, `psk4800`). Transmit exactly as Direwolf 1.7 `gen_tone.c` (read for interop):
1800 Hz constant-amplitude carrier with phase jumps at symbol boundaries, no shaping; HDLC bits NRZI-coded, MSB
first into symbols. QPSK: 1200 sym/s, Gray(dibit) x 90 deg + 45 deg per symbol (V.26 alternative B, Direwolf's
default). 8PSK: 1600 sym/s, V.27 Gray table x 45 deg.

Receiver (iterated through five measured problems):
1. Low-pass to remove 2fc after mixing added ISI that closed the 8PSK eye (open for 1-2 of 16 strobe phases).
2. A Hilbert front end left a ~1e-3 BER floor on *clean* 8PSK: at 1600 bd on 1800 Hz a symbol is 1.125 carrier
   cycles, so analytic-signal edge transients and non-orthogonal cos/sin windows corrupt every symbol.
   **Fix: per-symbol least-squares fit of A cos(wn + phi)** (cos/sin projections corrected by their 2x2 Gram
   matrix over the exact window, vectorised through running sums) -> clean BER 0 for both modes.
3. Gardner timing too jittery for the narrow 8PSK eye -> early-late gate on the fit magnitude.
4. Re-acquisition: coarse timing search at each burst onset, minimising the spread of |y| (constant envelope:
   uniform only when aligned); onsets at 2.5 ms resolution (Direwolf leaves ~10 ms between packets) relative to a
   tracked noise floor (an absolute threshold never re-armed once noise filled the gaps); plus re-acquisition
   when the mean decision residual nears a random decision's (a "within a quarter step" test is ~0.5 for random
   decisions too and fired on merely noisy blocks); acquire at start (input may begin mid-signal).
5. Knob-turning on the noise-floor rise rate made things worse; an acquisition-event log showed the remaining
   QPSK losses at 3000 rms were plain bit errors at the 8 kHz noise edge, not acquisition -- reverted.

## Notes

- 8PSK at 1600 bd on 1800 Hz is wideband relative to its carrier: an SSB mistuning (analytic-signal shift) of
  only 10 Hz leaves ~1.5e-2 BER for any receiver (matching the receiver's carrier did not help). It is a mode
  for flat-audio FM / VoIP links, not SSB; on VoIP the carrier never shifts.
- At 8 kHz our QPSK reaches its edge around 3000 rms noise (~17 dB Es/N0), a few dB short of ideal DQPSK.
- On the HF simulator 2400 QPSK collapses even on the CCIR "good" channel (0.5 ms delay = 60% of a symbol; a
  notch every 2 kHz) while AFSK 1200 survives -- single-carrier fast PSK without an equaliser is not an HF mode,
  which is what ARDOP's slower/multi-carrier modes address.
