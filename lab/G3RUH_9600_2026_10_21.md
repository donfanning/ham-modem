# Phase 6: 9600 bd G3RUH over a wideband VoIP leg — 2026-10-21

`hammodem/modems/g3ruh.py`: NRZI -> scrambler 1+x^12+x^17 (Direwolf's bit order) -> raised cosine (beta 0.5)
at an internal 48 kHz, decimated to the leg rate. Receive: interpolate to 48 kHz, low-pass, running-mean DC
removal, slicer + PLL + descrambler + NRZI + HDLC. Refuses 8 kHz (G.711 cannot carry 9600 bd baseband).

## Offline (40 frames, synthetic impairments)

| fs | clean | noise 2000 rms | 4000 | 6000 | +500 ppm + 2000 | +3000 DC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 kHz | 40 | 40 | 36 | 4 | 40 | 40 |
| 48 kHz | 40 | 40 | 40 | 39 | 40 | 40 |

CPU ~1.2% of a core at either rate.

## Direwolf 1.6 interop

| Direction | Result |
|---|---|
| ours -> `atest -B 9600`, 48 kHz / 16 kHz | 50/50, 50/50 |
| `gen_packets -B 9600 -r 48000 -n 100` noise ramp | ours 59 (was 53 before tuning), Direwolf 65 |
| `gen_packets -B 9600 -r 16000 -n 100` | ours 1, Direwolf 1 (gen_packets' 16 kHz output is not decodable by either) |

Receive-filter tuning is rate-dependent: native 48 kHz is best with a wide, short low-pass (0.8 x baud,
4 symbols); interpolated 16 kHz needs a narrower, longer filter (0.6 x baud, 8 symbols) -- 36 vs 28 frames at
4000 rms -- because the image and in-band noise of the 16 kHz leg dominate.

## Live, lab PBX

- Echo extension 7399, L16/16000 negotiated by Asterisk (SDP `L16/16000`, dynamic PT): 3/3 self-decoded.
- Room 7316 (`ham_bridge_wb`, 16 kHz mixing), 3 stations x 8 frames, TXDELAY 100 ms: **48/48, bad FCS 0**.

## Caveat

A 9600 station that dials the narrowband room (7300) still gets L16 negotiated, but the 8 kHz mix destroys the
signal silently. 9600 bd must use a wideband room. A later improvement: refuse or warn if the received audio
has no energy above 4 kHz.
